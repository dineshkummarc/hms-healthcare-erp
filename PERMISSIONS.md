# Permissions Matrix & Access Control Specification

## Access Hierarchy
- **Super Administrator**: Complete platform access across all organizations and modules.
- **Organization Owner**: Unrestricted control within their organization and child branches.
- **Hospital / Branch Administrator**: Operational management of branch facilities, schedules, and staff.
- **Doctor**: Scoped access to assigned department patients, outpatient consultation queues, EMR records, and electronic prescriptions.
- **Nurse**: Inpatient ward charts, vital signs recording, medication administration logs.
- **Pharmacist**: Pharmacy POS dispensing, inventory batches, and purchase orders.
- **Lab Technician**: Lab orders, specimen collection verification, and test result entry.
- **Accountant**: Invoices, payment receipts, cashier reconciliation, and general ledger journal entries.
- **Patient**: Scoped strictly to their own personal record, upcoming appointments, prescriptions, and billing receipts.
